# Bat-Clawd in the Knightmare: the suit

Research note, 2026-10-09. Scope: what Ben Affleck's Batman actually wears in the Knightmare, the vision of a ruined future in *Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice* (2016) and the epilogue of *Zack Snyder's Justice League* (2021), and how it becomes a Bat-Clawd outfit map on the prototype grid (`prototype/clawd-outfits`, `ClawdOutfits.prototype.tsx`). It replaces outfit K (the grey Batsuit with the big black bat, and the armour when alarmed) and reopens the Alarm color in [`snyder.md`](snyder.md) §3. It keeps the approved model of outfits V and I: a suit over the body, arms and legs, the cowl down to under the eyes, Clawd orange only as the jaw, white eyes, a neutral rim at rest, the back of the garment hanging behind the legs, and the aura only while alarmed.

## TL;DR

- **The outfit is desert gear worn over the Batsuit, with no cape.** It is a long, sleeved, light-brown leather duster worn open, a frayed brown scarf, flying goggles pushed up on the cowl, the cowl itself, the dark, battle-worn suit underneath (the chest bat is covered), a utility belt with a tan webbing belt and a thigh holster, olive cargo pants with knee pads, black forearm gauntlets with olive wraps, and black boots. He carries a rifle on a sling. Both films use the same look; Snyder's 2021 photo is "the same coat he wore in the Knightmare sequence" ([ScreenCrush, 4 Feb 2021](https://screencrush.com/justice-league-snyder-cut-batman-first-look/)).
- **Bat-Clawd wears a coat, not a cape.** The coat's sleeves cover the arms. Its front panels hang beside the outer legs, and its back hangs behind the legs, so the gaps between them show the coat's dark lining. That is how the "cape passes behind the legs" rule carries over.
- **What survives at 54px:** the coat's silhouette and colour, the goggles as a brass band across the forehead, and the cowl with its ears. The scarf, belt and pants read only as bands, and only from 72px. The chest emblem, wraps, holster and rifle are left out.
- **Alarmed:** the coat swings open and the aura comes on. **The armour is dropped**: it is the Gotham fight's suit, not the Knightmare's (§6).
- **Alarm color: Heat Vision `#e60f00`** (proposed), a scarlet in place of the poster's carmine `#e51232`. The Knightmare's enemy is Superman, so its red should be his: heat vision, and the "S" on his regime's soldiers. It keeps carmine's distances: 16.1 ΔE00 from Clawd orange and at least 11.7 from every sibling Alarm color. The runner-up, Omega fire amber `#ed8e12` (sampled), fits the footage best but collides with the Theme's dust waiting tier (§7).

## 1. What the Knightmare outfit is

**The written record.** Costume designer Michael Wilkinson confirmed at CCXP 2015 in São Paulo that the desert scene is "um pesadelo de Bruce Wayne". In Omelete's account of the panel (the reporter's paraphrase, not a quote), it is a post-apocalyptic desert "no estilo de *Mad Max*", where Superman has become a despot with an army of soldiers and flying creatures ([Omelete, Bruno Silva](https://www.omelete.com.br/batman/batman-vs-superman-a-origem-da-justica/ccxp-2015-figurinista-de-batman-vs-superman-confirma-pesadelo-de-bruce-wayne)). An English write-up quotes him on "a Mad Max quality to it … then of course Superman and his minions come". That line reached this note through a search summary; the page ([We Got This Covered](https://wegotthiscovered.com/?p=477400)) returned 403. A review the week of release says Batman is "now dressed in desert gear" ([ScreenCrush, 25 Mar 2016](https://screencrush.com/batman-vs-superman-spoilers-discussion/)). For the 2021 epilogue, Wilkinson says they "created a post-apocalyptic look for all of the characters" by "aging and embellishing versions of each character's costume" and "adding textures and various layering pieces" ([Rotten Tomatoes, Erik Amaya](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/gallery/creating-the-costumes-for-zack-snyders-justice-league-gallery)). Snyder posted a close-up of the costume on Vero with the caption "Joker Card" ([ComicBookMovie, 4 Dec 2016](https://www.comicbookmovie.com/batman_vs_superman/zack-snyder-shares-a-close-up-look-at-batman-v-supermans-a147271)). In February 2021 he posted the Batman of the epilogue "in the same coat he wore in the Knightmare sequence" ([ScreenCrush, 4 Feb 2021](https://screencrush.com/justice-league-snyder-cut-batman-first-look/)). No interview found names the coat, the scarf or the goggles piece by piece.

**What the official trailers show** (read frame by frame at 360p; times approximate):

- *BvS* Comic-Con Trailer, [Warner Bros., `0WWzgGyAH6Y`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WWzgGyAH6Y). At ~2:03–2:07, Superman stands in a lit doorway of the bunker with kneeling helmeted soldiers. At ~2:38–2:39, Batman stands behind barbed wire in a long coat; the cowl ears are clear and there is no cape. At ~2:40–2:41, soldiers in grey-black gear carry a red patch on the shoulder. The red is seen in the frame; ScreenCrush describes the patch as an "'S' insignia".
- *BvS* Official Trailer 2, [Warner Bros., `fis-9Zqu2Ro`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fis-9Zqu2Ro). At ~2:01, Batman in the coat brawls with those soldiers among army trucks, with winged creatures overhead. The coat swings out as a heavy skirt.
- *BvS* Official Final Trailer, [Warner Bros., `NhWg7AQLI_8`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NhWg7AQLI_8). At ~1:55, a long shot: Batman, a dark column in a coat to the ankles, stands on a ridge above a carved plain ringed by burning pits, the Omega. The fire is the only saturated colour in the frame.
- *Zack Snyder's Justice League* Official Trailer, [Warner Bros. UK & Ireland, `ui37YKQ9AC4`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ui37YKQ9AC4). At ~0:35–0:37, his profile against a burning amber sky and an alien ship: short cowl ears, the goggles up on the forehead, the coat's turned-up collar, and a sling strap across the chest. At ~2:04–2:06, a close-up: the cowl to under the eyes, round goggles with smoked lenses pushed up, and the scarf and coat at the neck. The Rotten Tomatoes gallery's Knightmare still is the same close-up.

**Merchandise, used only to confirm shapes and colours.** Hot Toys made the costume twice:

- **MMS372** (2016, *BvS*): "light-brown colored long coat", two brown scarves (one for the lower face), "black forearm gauntlets with green arm wraps", "dark green pants with knee pads", black boots, a utility belt, a second belt with a thigh holster, goggles, binoculars, a machine gun with strap, and a pistol ([Sideshow 902770](https://www.sideshow.com/collectibles/dc-comics-knightmare-batman-hot-toys-902770/)).
- **TMS038** (2021, *ZSJL*, with Black Suit Superman): the same list, plus a "Batsuit with battle-damaged effects" and the coat "embedded with bendable wire" ([Sideshow 908013](https://www.sideshow.com/collectibles/dc-comics-knightmare-batman-and-superman-hot-toys-908013); [Toyark, 18 Mar 2021](https://news.toyark.com/2021/03/18/zack-snyders-justice-league-knightmare-batman-and-black-suit-superman-set-by-hot-toys-420821)).

Neither list has a cape or a tactical vest. Mattel's DC Multiverse figure also has a trench coat and goggles ([Action Figure Barbecue](https://www.actionfigurebarbecue.com/2016/05/action-figure-review-knightmare-batman.html)). In the figure photos the suit's chest bat is a dark relief that the scarf and the coat's lapels mostly hide.

**Cape or coat: coat.** Every frame and both figures show a coat and no cape. A cape would bring back the generic Batsuit the maintainer rejected.

**How "the cape passes behind the legs" translates.** A duster hangs from the shoulders all the way round. Its front panels fall outside the outer legs, and its back panel falls behind them. On the map:

- At rest and flying, the front edges are map cells at columns 3 and 12 down to y = 10, and the boots show below the hem on the last half-row.
- The gaps between the legs (columns 5, 7–8 and 10) show the lining `D` from a rectangle behind the map, never the ground. This rectangle is what makes the rule hold.
- When alarmed, the front panels swing out with the coat, so columns 3 and 12 of the leg rows open onto the lining as well.

Draw order, behind the map: in flying, `COAT_TRAIL` in `C`, then `COAT_LINING` in `D`. In alarmed, `COAT_BACK` in `D`, then `COAT_OPEN` and its mirror in `C`. The neutral rim `rgb(232 225 217 / 22%)` at 0.35 is stroked on the coat paths through the same body-box mask as `suitCape`, so it never draws over the body. The rim also goes on `COWL_EDGE` at 0.7. The goggles need no edge of their own. Both coat paths start at the prototype's conventions: the trail's `M3 5 L13 5` and the open panel's `M3.4 5`. `suitCape` can't be reused as it is. It hardcodes `fill="#000"` on the panels and the alarmed back rect, and draws nothing between the legs in flight. The coat needs a `coatBehind` variant: `suitCape` with fill parameters for the panels (`C`) and the back (`D`), plus `COAT_LINING` in flying mode.

**Direction.** The trail streams **left**, like every cape in the prototype. The dossier's Signature pose (§[7](snyder.md)) blows the coat and the ash to the **right**. The maintainer should pick one. If right, mirror `COAT_TRAIL` with `MIRROR`.

At 54px (rendered preview), the figure reads as a cowl with a brass band across the forehead, white eyes, an orange jaw, and a brown coat around a dark centre. In flight the coat streams out like a heavy flag. Alarmed, it flares into a trapezoid, and the aura sits on the coat's outline.

## 5. The aura

Same mechanism as the prototype: `alarmAura(rgb)`, two drop-shadows at 1.5px / 90% and 5px / 55%, only while alarmed or in an iconic moment. With the proposal below, the call is `alarmAura('230 15 0')`.

## 6. Decisions this note makes

- **The armour is dropped as the alarmed Pose.** In outfit K and the dossier, Bat-Clawd switches to the armoured suit when alarmed. That suit belongs to the rain-soaked Gotham fight under the Bat-Signal, not to the desert. Mixing the two is part of what made K read as "the grey Batsuit". Alarmed is now the same Knightmare figure with the coat open and the aura on. If the maintainer wants the armour back, it fits better as a one-off iconic moment than as a Pose.
- **Overruled by the maintainer (2026-10-09): a chest bat is required, see "Maintainer's review" below.** The original reasoning was: **No chest emblem.** The suit underneath has the *BvS* bat, but in every frame and both figures the scarf and the coat's lapels cover it. At 54px a dark bat on the charcoal centre would not read anyway. This drops the dossier's "the chest bat shows between the coat's panels".
- **No rifle**, by the dossier's "No weapons" rule (§[12](snyder.md)). **No binoculars**: they would cover the eyes.
- **The goggles stay up on the cowl**, never over the eyes, as in the *ZSJL* frames, the official still and both figures. The white eyes stay visible in every Pose. This drops the dossier's "the white eyes stay as the two lenses".

## 7. The Alarm color

The dossier's carmine `#e51232` comes from the poster's logo, which is marketing, not the Knightmare. The brief's candidates, measured (ΔE00; "dust ink" is the Theme's waiting tier `#b8a383`; "Nolan" is the WATCHFUL PROTECTOR draft `#ff5340`; "2022" is the default crimson `#af0006`; "Burton" is the PALE MOONLIGHT gold `#f0c24b`):

| Candidate | Hex | Status | Hue | Contrast / ground | vs Clawd | vs dust ink | vs carmine | vs Nolan | vs 2022 | vs Burton |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carmine (today) | `#e51232` | sampled, poster | 351° | 4.16 | 17.1 | 35.5 | — | 11.7 | 13.1 | 48.9 |
| **Heat Vision** | **`#e60f00`** | **proposed** | 4° | 4.13 | **16.1** | 34.1 | 8.7 | **11.7** | 11.9 | 44.6 |
| Omega fire | `#ed8e12` | sampled, *BvS* final trailer | 34° | 7.90 | 18.2 | **19.1** | 33.9 | 25.0 | 38.2 | 17.0 |
| Parademon ember | `#d4521e` | proposed, from the fire's red end | 17° | 4.68 | **10.2** | 27.3 | 13.8 | 11.0 | 16.2 | 36.3 |
| Desert dust | `#b8a383` | the dossier's dust | 36° | 8.02 | 20.4 | **0** | 35.5 | 28.9 | 41.4 | 18.0 |

A good Alarm color has to stay clear of Clawd orange, of the Theme's own waiting tier, and of the other Themes' alarms.

- **Desert dust** fails outright: it *is* the waiting tier. It is also the colour of the whole world in these frames, so it cannot mean "look here".
- **Parademon ember** sits 10.2 from Clawd orange. A glow that close to the jaw would read as Clawd glowing, not as an alarm.
- **Omega fire** is the strongest film anchor. It is the one saturated colour in the *BvS* long shot, and the *ZSJL* sky burns the same amber. It is also the most legible (7.90:1, so it could even be text). But it shares hue (34° vs 36°) and lightness (L* 67.6 vs 68.0) with the dust ink. On the cards, AUTHORIZE and NEW INTEL would differ only in chroma. Adopting it means moving the waiting tier off dust, for example to a grey ash, and redoing the dossier's stamp logic. The dossier also rejected "the Knightmare's orange fire" for crowding Clawd orange. The numbers don't bear that out (18.2 ΔE, more than carmine's 17.1). The real collision is with the dust.
- **Heat Vision `#e60f00` is the recommendation.** The Knightmare is the world under Superman, and red is his mark in it: the eyes, and the "S" on his soldiers' shoulders (seen in the Comic-Con trailer at ~2:40). Moving from carmine's pinkish 351° to a hot scarlet at 4° re-anchors the colour in the story. It loses none of carmine's distances: 16.1 from Clawd, 11.7 from Nolan's draft, 11.9 from the 2022 crimson, and 34.1 from the dust. Against the brown coat the aura is 27.1 ΔE away, so it outlines the figure clearly. The hue comes from the sampled bloom, but it is pushed to full saturation, so it is **proposed**, not sampled. The heat-vision frame is from the Gotham fight, not the Knightmare (Unverified).

**Knock-on effects in the dossier**, if Heat Vision is adopted: `--signal-hot` becomes `#e60f00`, and `--signal` its darker body, about `#b80c00` (**proposed**; L* 48.5 → 38.6, the same drop as carmine `#e51232` → `#b8121f`). That body sits 2.7 ΔE from 2022's `#af0006`, against 4.7 for today's `#b8121f`. Large fills always carry the `--signal-hot` rim and the Knightmare's grey ground, so the Themes still separate, but check it next to 2022 on screen. `--ink-hot` `#ff4d57` can stay: it already passes on `--raised` (4.81:1), and lifting the new red toward text would land on Nolan's `#ff5340`. Like carmine, the new red fails as text on the ground (4.13:1), so the rule "pixels and thin rules only" carries over. The dust tier is untouched. A variant to judge on screen: run the inner 1.5px shadow in the white-hot core `#ffb8ab` and the outer one in `#e60f00`, so the aura glows like the eyes. No other Theme's aura uses two colours.

## 8. Legal

No film image, still, trailer frame or merchandise photo goes into the repo. This note describes them and links to their official sources. The hexes are measurements, and the outfit is an original pixel drawing of shapes (a coat, goggles, a scarf) common to the genre, on Claude Code's own mascot. The Theme's name and credit stay as the dossier sets them (§[12](snyder.md)).

## Unverified items

- Whether the *ZSJL* coat is the very same garment as *BvS*'s. One source says "the same coat" (ScreenCrush, 2021); TMS038's scarf is darker than MMS372's.
- The coat's true pigment under neutral light: the film anchors are graded and backlit, and the merchandise anchors are studio-lit.
- Whether the *BvS* bat is on the suit under the scarf in the films themselves, not just on the figures.
- Whether Superman's heat vision appears in either Knightmare sequence. The only heat-vision frame here is from the Gotham fight in the *BvS* final trailer.
- The colour of the regime's "S" patch: seen, but too small to sample at 360p.
- Wilkinson's English "Mad Max quality" quote: the outlet returned 403, so it is taken from a search summary. The Portuguese account (Omelete) is fetched but is a paraphrase.
- The art book *Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice – The Art of the Film* was not consulted.

## Maintainer's review (2026-10-09)

After round 5 of the prototype (#95), approved with two changes for the build (#83):

1. **A chest bat.** The *BvS* bat is drawn on the suit's chest between the coat's lapels and under the scarf, from the theme's own emblem path, so the Knightmare still reads as Batman at 54px. It needs enough value against the charcoal suit to read (a darker bat on a slightly lighter chest, or a faint rim), never the Alarm color at rest.
2. **Arms and the torso's sides need contrast against the coat.** Flying, the near arm sits on the trailing coat in the same brown and disappears, so he looks one-armed. The sleeves and the torso's sides take a clear value step from the coat behind them (darker sleeves, or a dark seam between body and coat panels), so both arms read at 54px in every pose.
