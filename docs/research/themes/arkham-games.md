For FREEFLOW: the amber stays as a recorded exception, with the dark-glass L1 disc; green as drafted; both crouches stay and the searchlights go to TAS, so the Games keep plain rain; the grapnel is the Games' (Lego's flying pose leaves it), and the alarmed counter moves away from West's fight (§9, decisions 3, 5, 11, 14).

Research note, 2026-10-22. Scope: one Bat-Signal Theme after Rocksteady's trilogy, *Batman: Arkham Asylum* (2009), *Batman: Arkham City* (2011) and *Batman: Arkham Knight* (2015), taken as one work (maintainer, LUC-85). It covers palette, type, Voice, Lexicon, Bat-Clawd, the emblem, the disc and the Atmosphere, measured against the seven shipped Themes, the paused SERIOUS HOUSE, and Clawd orange. Wave 3 rules are in [`wave-3-brief.md`](wave-3-brief.md); the dossier's form is [`wave-2-brief.md`](wave-2-brief.md) §6. Terms are those of [`CONTEXT.md`](../../../CONTEXT.md).

## 1. TL;DR

- **The Theme in one sentence:** Rocksteady's Gotham at night, lit green from above, where the games' own highlight colour, the amber that marks what is selected, new or usable in Asylum's menus and detective mode, marks what needs you. Bat-Clawd wears the armoured dark-grey suit and keeps watch crouched on a gargoyle.
- **Display name:** **FREEFLOW** (recommended), Rocksteady's own name for the combat system in all three games ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum), [City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_City), [Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Knight)). Alternatives: **SILENT PREDATOR** (the stealth half of every game) or **KNIGHTFALL** (Knight's ending protocol; it is also a 1993 comic arc, which confuses). **Credit:** "Rocksteady's Arkham trilogy" (a no-break space before "trilogy", as the shipped credits do), or "Rocksteady's Arkham games", which says "game" outright; the maintainer picks. Either names a studio, not authors, so the picker tells it from SERIOUS HOUSE's "Arkham Asylum, by Morrison and McKean" at a glance (§3, §11).
- **What is the games' and not the book's.** The armoured suit (Jim Lee's build, "military influences and an industrial look"), the gauntlet fins, the pouch belt, the cape worn over the shoulders; the gargoyle perch and the grapnel; the HUD, its menus and its "NEW OBJECTIVE"; detective mode's *highlights*; the open city (City's walls, Knight's rain). The book's blue-black, scarlet, hand lettering, scratches, needle ears, hooked cape and horror stay with SERIOUS HOUSE. The Joker and his funhouse ([30]) are left out on purpose.
- **Green: a split, for the maintainer to confirm.** The games take green as their **ground** (Asylum's green nights, Knight's green-lit streets) and a pale green *waiting* ink; the book keeps its green *working* ink (`#86d66a`), 12.5 away. SERIOUS HOUSE's own record found the book never uses green as a ground. Four options are in §4.6, including one with no green ink here.
- **Alarm: Asylum's amber `#ffad00`** (signal `#e08f00`), sampled flat off the menus, map, WayneTech and detective mode ([15], [16], [18], [19], [22]). It was the second option until round 1's claims arrived: the first, a detective orange `#fe7400` searched for the widest gap, sits **0.3 ΔE00 from The Long Halloween's jack-o'-lantern `#fe7500`** and 6.7 from The Animated Series' red sky `#ff6420`. Only one orange can stay; the amber leaves it to them (§4.3). Waiting ink: the asylum's green light `#b3e8b8`; working ink: bone.
- **Detective mode without WATCHFUL PROTECTOR's look:** the games' detective vision "tints the game world blue and highlights interactive objects" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum)). The blue tint and the sonar ring stay WATCHFUL PROTECTOR's. This Theme takes only the *highlight*: an amber edge on what needs you (the lit disc's bat, the urgent card).
- **Type:** Oxanium (OFL-1.1), squared techno capitals like City's and Knight's WayneTech menus, for display and stamps. Teko and Staatliches are the alternatives (§5).
- **Signature pose:** **The gargoyle.** Bat-Clawd lands and crouches on the perch, the cape falling over the ledge on one side ([23], [24], [34], [36], [56]). No shipped Signature is a crouch; its outline is about two units lower than any stand and lopsided at the foot. **Round 1 conflict:** The Animated Series' S1 is also a crouch (a symmetric dome, the cape spread both sides). The outlines differ, but two crouches is the maintainer's call; this dossier's fallback is the grapnel ascent (§8.3).
- **Atmosphere:** rain falling almost straight down, pale green-white, a green haze from above (the `rain` painter, no engine work). Optional engine ask: City's searchlight beams crossing the sky ([29], [01], [56]); The Animated Series also asks for sweeping searchlights (a blimp's), so only one Theme should get them.
- **Known costs, recorded:** the surface sits 6.1 ΔE00 from BECOME A BAT's (at the ~6 floor); the amber alarm sits 9.2 from PALE MOONLIGHT's gold alarm (under the ~10 floor, like BECOME A BAT's 6.1 exception) and 5.9 from VENGEANCE's *working* amber. Silhouette scores `~` against VENGEANCE, PALE MOONLIGHT and BECOME A BAT (§11); the Theme still clears 3 C axes against everyone.

## 3. Visual identity and sources

What makes the trilogy recognisable, in order of strength:

1. **The armoured suit.** "Batman's design was heavily influenced by the work of comic artist Jim Lee… His black and dark gray costume was based on modern versions, and has military influences and an industrial look" ([Wikipedia, Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum)). Read on [04], [05], [33]: a dark-grey armoured body, a black cowl, gloves and cape, a wide bat on the chest in a darker tone of the suit, a pouch belt, and three blades on each forearm. City keeps it ([23], [24], [33]). Knight rebuilds it to "match that of the Batmobile" and adds "armor over Batman's shoulders, covering the previously exposed cape" ([Wikipedia, Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Knight)); [46] and [47] show black plates with the bat cut into the chest plates.
2. **Predator: gargoyles, the grapnel, the glide.** "Many areas feature stone gargoyles placed high above… Batman can use his grapnel gun to reach the gargoyles… From the gargoyles, Batman can glide down to attack enemies or hang upside down" ([Wikipedia, Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum)). City's box art and key art put him on a gargoyle ([23], [24]); the title screen, a press shot and Knight's gameplay shot crouch him on a ledge ([26], [34], [56]).
3. **Saturated light in a dark world.** "Browns and monochromatic colors could depict the desired dark and moody atmosphere, but the developers wanted the aesthetic to resemble the vibrant color schemes of a comic book. To this end, they used saturated colors for in-game lighting… to highlight points of interest" ([Wikipedia, Asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Asylum)). Measured: Asylum's nights are green-black ([09], [11], [12]), Knight's green to teal ([46], [54]), City's blue-grey ([32], [35]).
4. **The HUD and detective vision.** Detective vision is "a visual mode which provides contextual information, tinting the game world blue and highlighting interactive objects" (same). Measured on [15] and [39]: the highlights are amber and orange. The menus use the same amber in Asylum ([16]–[19], [22]), green and teal in City ([40]–[42]), teal with orange in Knight ([57], [60]).
5. **FreeFlow.** All three articles name the combat "Freeflow": Asylum's "lets Batman move quickly between enemies, chaining attacks" (Asylum); City uses "an improved version" (City); Knight's "allows for basic attacks, including strikes, counters, and dodging" (Knight).
6. **Places.** The island asylum, gothic and Victorian ([10], [21]); City's walled prison, its wall "patrolled by TYGER operatives" ([37]) and its winter; Knight's three islands with "the neon-tinged Chinatown" and a Halloween night of rain ([48], [53], [58]; [Wikipedia, Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman:_Arkham_Knight)).

Credits: Rocksteady Studios, published by Eidos (Asylum) and Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment; written by Paul Dini (Asylum, City, with Paul Crocker and Sefton Hill) and by Sefton Hill, Ian Ball and Martin Lancaster (Knight); Batman voiced by Kevin Conroy (all three articles).

**What the Theme takes from each game.** Asylum: the suit, the menu amber, the green nights, the gargoyle. City: the gargoyle crouch, the HUD's objective "!", the walls, the searchlights. Knight: the rain, the HUD orange, the plated suit as an option. The Batmobile ([43], [55]) is left out: at 54px it would be a second mascot.

### 4.3 The Alarm color: Asylum amber

In all three games the warm highlight marks what you can act on: amber `#ffad00` in Asylum's detective mode and menus ([15], [16]), orange `#fca458` on City's detective-mode gargoyles ([39]), orange `#f95311`–`#fa8e2d` in Knight's HUD and WayneTech ([57], [60]). That is this Theme's "needs you". Two real options, and round 1 decided between them:

- **Amber `#ffad00` (recommended).** Asylum's menu amber, exactly the sampled UI colour (LCh 77 83 76): the selection, "NEW", the map's route, detective mode's labels and outlines. Fill `--signal` `#e08f00`; `--ink-hot` the same amber (7.63:1 at worst, no lift needed). Costs: **9.2 from PALE MOONLIGHT's gold alarm** (under the jury's ~10 floor; BECOME A BAT's 6.1 from KNIGHTMARE is the one recorded exception; the two Themes would be told apart by green against smog brown and in the picker) and **5.9 from VENGEANCE's working amber** (an alarm against another Theme's running-work ink). 26.6 from Clawd, so the aura stays clear of his jaw.
- **Orange `#fe7400` (the first pick, now contested).** Proposed between Knight's two oranges, then placed by a search over the warm half of the wheel for the largest gap to every shipped ink and to Clawd (nearest: Clawd 13.0, WATCHFUL PROTECTOR's sodium 13.6, PALE MOONLIGHT's bronze 13.7). Fill `#d65a00`, ink `#ff8420`. Round 1 made it unworkable as it stands: **0.3 ΔE00 from The Long Halloween's `#fe7500`** (the same colour, found the same way) and 6.7 from The Animated Series' option A `#ff6420`. It is also the closest alarm to Bat-Clawd's orange (13.0). Keep it only if the maintainer gives the orange to the games and moves the other two.

**The HUD's objective green `#57fd6d` (rejected)** as the alarm, on the HUD's teal-black. Its numbers: grounds 3.9 / 3.6 from ANCIENT GRACE (teal grounds are ANCIENT GRACE's), the alarm 9.8 from SERIOUS HOUSE's green working ink, and green reads as "all fine" to a tired developer. It stays a footnote.

### 4.4 Inks

- **Waiting (`--ink-soft`): the asylum's green light `#b3e8b8`** ([12] bright3, the green sky over Crime Alley; LCh 87 32 146). With amber as the alarm, the waiting tier can't be warm: every warm ink left sits within ~5 of The Long Halloween's or The Animated Series' orange (our former `#ff8a3d` is 5.1 from `#fe7500`). Costs: 12.5 from SERIOUS HOUSE's Joker-green *working* ink (cross-role), and green on the waiting tier can read as "fine" (SERIOUS HOUSE's own note raised this). The stamp's word, box and side rule carry the tier, so colour is not the only cue; decide on screen. Fallback, if the maintainer wants no green ink here: City's pale HUD text `#c5d291` ([38]), 7.8 from BECOME A BAT's canary and 9.1 from SERIOUS HOUSE's ochre, both waiting inks.
- **Working (`--ink-live`): Bone** `#e9f0ea`, the safe choice: KNIGHTMARE, BECOME A BAT and ANCIENT GRACE already use bone for running work (4.5–12.1 from theirs; bone is shared on purpose).
- With the orange option instead, the waiting ink was the amber `#ffad00` and the working ink bone or the green light.
- **Bone and the greys** carry a faint green, like everything under the asylum's light: bone `#e9f0ea`, ink `#c3d0c6`, ash `#8fa196`.

### 4.5 Grounds

Asylum's green-black ([09] `#181e18` h144, [11] `#171c15` h136) moved toward Knight's ([54] `#223128` h156) and darkened: **surface `#0b1a10`** (L* 7.7, C* 9.4, h 153), **raised `#11301c`** (L* 17.1, **C* 19.6**, h 151). The jury's floor for a green ground was C* 15 or more on the raised tone (`jury-wave-2.md` §8); this clears it. Against the green-family neighbours: BECOME A BAT's olive 6.1 / 10.9 (surface at the ~6 floor, the cost), ANCIENT GRACE's icy blue 11.0 / 16.3. Since ANCIENT GRACE moved off tank grey to icy blue (jury §9 follow-ups), the green between them is free.

## 12. Legal note

- **Owners:** Batman, Gotham, the characters and the bat symbol are DC's; the games are Rocksteady's, published by Warner Bros. Games (Eidos for Asylum). Homage only (ADR 0001).
- **Not used:** any game logo or wordmark (the bat with "BATMAN" and the subtitles, [01], [21], [44]); any UI asset (Asylum's ink-splatter boxes, City's "W" WayneTech icon, Knight's hexagon icons, the Riddler trophy, the detective-mode skeletons); any screenshot, texture, model or traced frame; the Batmobile; the music; Kevin Conroy's or Mark Hamill's voices or lines; the names "WayneTech" and "TYGER" in the UI.
- **Ours:** the chest bat, redrawn from the suit as a vector path and credited to DC as a trademark like every Theme's emblem; the gargoyle, if drawn, a generic gothic one; every Voice line.
- **Generic:** armour, gauntlet fins, a pouch belt, gargoyles, rain, searchlights, amber highlights, the words "objective", "mission", "incoming".
- **The display name:** FREEFLOW is Rocksteady's name for a game system, used as an allusion. Whether it is a registered mark is unverified (§13); SILENT PREDATOR and KNIGHTFALL are the fallbacks.
- **Fonts:** Oxanium, Teko and Staatliches are OFL-1.1; ship their licences with the others.
- **Respect:** the games' asylum, its patients and its villains are not mocked or shown; the Voice keeps SERIOUS HOUSE's respect list.

## 13. Unverified items

- **The games' typefaces** are not identified by any source we read; the faces in §5 evoke them.
- **[04]**, the Asylum character artwork, is given as official by the Batman Wiki; we did not find it on a publisher page. It is the clearest view of the suit; the in-game [05] and [33] agree with it.
- **[35], [36] and [46] are not publisher images.** Nothing in the palette depends on them.
- **Screenshots are rendered and compressed**; Steam's crops are 1280×720 for Asylum and City. Sampled grounds are the look, not texture values. The UI colours (`#ffad00`, `#57fd6d`) sample flat and are reliable.
- **The counter indicator** over an attacker's head, the inverted hang (P3) and Knight's rain lasting the whole night are from memory or from text only; we found no capture of the first two.
- **The chest bat's proportions** (about 3:1, two scallops a side) are read from three captures at angles, not measured on a frontal flat view.
- **FREEFLOW as a trademark** is unchecked.
- **Stamp widths** come from PIL on TTF files; the test reads the WOFF files (expected within a pixel).
- **The other Wave 3 dossiers** were not yet in the claims table when this was written.
