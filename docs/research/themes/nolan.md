# Theme dossier: Christopher Nolan's Dark Knight trilogy (2005–2012)

Research note, 2026-10-09. Scope: one Bat-Signal Theme after *Batman Begins* (2005), *The Dark Knight* (2008) and *The Dark Knight Rises* (2012), anchored on *The Dark Knight*. It covers palette, type, Voice, Lexicon, Bat-Clawd, the emblem, the Signal and the Atmosphere, measured against the default Theme, The Batman (2022), and the sibling dossiers in this folder. The terms are defined in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../../CONTEXT.md).

Revised 2026-10-09 after the wave 1 jury (`jury-wave-1.md` §10).

## 1. TL;DR

- **The Theme in one sentence:** a cold, modernist Gotham of steel-blue glass and lit office windows, where nothing is warm until something needs you and the bat catches fire.
- **Display name:** **WATCHFUL PROTECTOR**. **Credit:** "after Christopher Nolan's *Dark Knight* trilogy". The name alludes to Gordon's closing words in *The Dark Knight*, "He's a silent guardian — a watchful protector — a dark knight" ([Wikiquote](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_(film))).
- **This Theme owns the operations frame** (jury §10.2): Ops on the tab, CLEARANCE, stencil stamps, the mono log and the briefing Voice. No other Wave 1 Theme uses them.
- **Anchor film: *The Dark Knight*.** Its look is the trilogy's most distinct one: a "colder, more modern world" with a "slightly blue-green" tone ([AC, July 2008](https://forums.superherohype.com/threads/american-cinematographer-dark-knight-article-part-i.307097/)), Chicago modernism, and a poster where the bat symbol burns into a skyscraper. *Begins* adds the sodium and mercury streetlight pair. *Rises* adds nothing to the base look.
- **Decided after round 5 of the prototype (#95): Blue Flame `#3d8bff`, and Bat-Clawd's suit as in [`bat-clawd-nolan-suit.md`](bat-clawd-nolan-suit.md).** The palette rows and §7 below predate that decision; where they differ, that note wins. The earlier status was: **Alarm color: to be decided on screen**, at header size next to Bat-Clawd (jury §10.9). The draft is "Burning Bat", a fire red-orange (`#d9301a` large, `#ff5340` for small inks), sampled from the poster's fire. It sits 9.6 ΔE00 from Clawd orange, the closest alarm to the mascot in the Wave. If no red works there, this Theme may take an alarm that is not red. The candidates are in §3. Whatever wins, nothing warm appears at rest. Working is mercury blue-green, waiting is sodium amber.
- **Type:** two new families, both on Fontsource. **Syncopate** (Apache-2.0) gives the wide, letterspaced display caps. **Big Shoulders Stencil** (OFL) gives the stamps a stencilled-crate look, and it was made for the Chicago Design System. Big Shoulders is this Theme's alone: the Chicago shoot is the tie (jury §10.3). Barlow Semi Condensed and JetBrains Mono stay. Special Elite is dropped for this Theme.
- **Lexicon:** Ops on the tab, On you, CLEARANCE, FAULT, YOUR MOVE, CHECKED IN, NO CONTACT (jury §5).
- **Signature pose: *Sonar*, standing.** No source was found for a crouch, so he stands and the ring carries the pose (§7).

## 2. Visual identity and sources

**What makes this version recognisable**, strongest first:

1. **A real, modern city, often in daylight.** For *The Dark Knight*, Nolan wanted to "make things as bright as possible, even as the material gets darker". The first 66 days of the shoot were in Chicago, and the IMAX bank-heist prologue "unfolds in the daytime" (David Heuring, *American Cinematographer*, July 2008, read in a [SuperHeroHype repost](https://forums.superherohype.com/threads/american-cinematographer-dark-knight-article-part-i.307097/); the wording was not checked against the magazine). It was the first major film shot partly with IMAX cameras ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight)). Pfister wanted the IMAX sequences "sharper and clearer, with improved contrast" (AC repost, same).
2. **Pfister's three tones for *The Dark Knight*:** "one is slightly blue-green", "another is neutral, almost black-and-white", and "the third is a rust-like tinge that references *Batman Begins*". In the same article: "We wanted to suggest a colder, more modern world" ([AC repost](https://forums.superherohype.com/threads/american-cinematographer-dark-knight-article-part-i.307097/)). The ICG cover story gives the same split: "blue, green and pure white tones" against "very black and rusty hues" ([Bob Fisher, ICG Magazine, 1 July 2008](https://www.icgmagazine.com/web/july-cover-story-the-dark-knight/)). The film was timed "using the traditional photochemical process" with David Orr (AC repost) and finished in "traditional timing at Technicolor" ([ICG](https://www.icgmagazine.com/web/july-cover-story-the-dark-knight/)).
3. **Two streetlight colors (from *Begins*).** Pfister: "It was a matter of choosing the two primary urban colors: an orange, sodium-vapor look and a blue-green, mercury-vapor look". He lit with "1/2 Straw gels" for the first and "1/4 Blue and 1/2 Green gels" for the second. Gotham was lit from within its buildings: "Ninety percent of city lighting is about the lighting within buildings rather than exterior light" ([Stephen Pizzello, *American Cinematographer*, June 2005](https://theasc.com/article/batman-begins-the-bat-takes-wing/)). In 2008 he said "the red-yellow patina of *Batman Begins* came naturally from the sodium-vapor lamps" ([AC repost](https://forums.superherohype.com/threads/american-cinematographer-dark-knight-article-part-i.307097/)).
4. **Crowley's modernism.** He calls himself "a desperate modernist" and says *The Dark Knight* "is actually the Batman I always wanted to make" ([Collider, 29 July 2020](https://collider.com/the-dark-knight-trilogy-nathan-crowley-interview-batmobile-batpod-gotham/)). He found "the clean, neat lines of Chicagoan architecture enhanced the urban-crime drama" (Jesser & Pourroy, *The Art and Making of The Dark Knight Trilogy*, 2012, pp. 72, 74, as cited in [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight)). Metrograph summarises his reference as 1960s–70s modernist architecture and *The Parallax View*'s government buildings ([Filmcraft: Nathan Crowley](https://metrograph.com/category/filmcraft-nathan-crowley/); it is not clear whose words those are). The Batcave became "a brightly lit secret bunker", and "the entire ceiling of the bunker emits light" ([AC repost](https://forums.superherohype.com/threads/american-cinematographer-dark-knight-article-part-i.307097/)).
5. **A matte, tactical suit.** "Batman's costume is made of a non-reflective, black material", and "the cape was absolutely matte black" ([ICG](https://www.icgmagazine.com/web/july-cover-story-the-dark-knight/)). Pfister: "a very soft backlight created a nice foundation of light on him", and he chose "silhouetting Batman against backgrounds rather than trying to light the material up" ([AC 2005](https://theasc.com/article/batman-begins-the-bat-takes-wing/)). For 2008, Lindy Hemming, Crowley and Nolan redesigned the suit as "smaller plates of armor with open spaces", with a "neckpiece [that] was part of the suit and came up to meet the cowl" and a mask "modelled after a motorcycle helmet" ([Special effects of *The Dark Knight*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_effects_of_The_Dark_Knight), citing Jesser & Pourroy 2012 pp. 112–115 and *Entertainment Weekly*, 18 June 2007). In *Begins*, Lucius Fox's "Applied Science Division" supplies the gear ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Begins)).
6. **The burning bat.** On the theatrical one-sheet ([enwiki file](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Dark_Knight_(2008_film).jpg)), the bat symbol is a fire burned into a skyscraper over a steel-blue sky, and the title logo is a black bat on a cold blue-white glow (observed by us). The opening credits reportedly form the symbol from "large blue flames" (IMDb crazy credits, quoted in a search excerpt; the page returned 403, so **unverified**).
7. **Sound.** For *Begins*, "Zimmer wanted to avoid writing music that had been done in earlier Batman films" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Begins)). For the Joker in *The Dark Knight* he processed recordings to evoke "thunder and razors" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight)). The mood we take from this is restraint: few notes, held long. Sound is out of scope here.

**Why *The Dark Knight* anchors the Theme:**

- It is the trilogy's own look. Crowley: "we had to step over *Batman Begins* to get to it" ([Collider](https://collider.com/the-dark-knight-trilogy-nathan-crowley-interview-batmobile-batpod-gotham/)).
- *Begins*' "rusty-copper tone" ([ICG](https://www.icgmagazine.com/web/july-cover-story-the-dark-knight/)) would sit on Bat-Clawd's orange and on 2022's sodium amber.
- *Rises* is "meant to be winter in Gotham" (Emma Thomas, in [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_Rises)). Its grey-white look drifts toward Snyder, and the Burton dossier already claims snow.
- *The Dark Knight* keeps a "rust-like tinge" from *Begins*, which gives the amber waiting ink an honest source.
- The bat symbol is the same across the three films (§8), so the emblem does not depend on this choice.

### The Alarm color: Burning Bat

Fire is the hottest thing in this world, and it is the poster's only warm color. The problem is that the poster's core fire orange (`#df5c2c`, hue 15°) has the same hue as Bat-Clawd's body (`#d77757`, hue 15°). So the Alarm moves down to the deep-red end of the fire, at hue 7°. Bat-Clawd's body stays at 15° and 2022's Signal Red sits at 358°. The alarm alone doesn't separate this Theme from 2022.

**Status: to be decided on screen at header size next to Bat-Clawd; if no red works, a non-red alarm is allowed** (jury §4.9, §10.9). The jury measured the draft ink `#ff5340` at 9.6 ΔE00 from Clawd `#d77757`. Under "nothing warm at rest", the orange mascot is the only warm pixel left, so an alarm stamp beside it is the test. Candidates, each to be shown as a stamp next to the mascot in the header:

| Candidate | Large fill / small ink | Contrast of the ink (abyss / surface / raised) | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. **Burning Bat** (the draft), hue 7° | `#d9301a` / `#ff5340` | 6.31 / 5.69 / 4.98 | Closest alarm to Clawd orange in the Wave |
| B. **Deeper red**, toward hue 2° | `#e8261c` / `#ff4136` (P) | 5.83 / 5.25 / 4.60 | 14.3 ΔE00 from Clawd per the jury, but close to 2022's red; the steel ground and the cool inks carry the difference. The jury's default if the check is inconclusive |
| C. **Burning Bat with a rim** | as A | as A | Keep the hue; alarm pixels next to the mascot (the disc's count box, the alarmed Pose) get a 0.5-unit `--abyss` rim |
| D. **Blue flame** (not red) | about `#2f7fe0` / `#5aa9ff` (P) | 8.22 / 7.41 / 6.49 | After the opening titles' blue flames (**unverified**, §12). Far from Clawd and from every other Wave alarm. But blue reads as "info" in most UIs, and mercury blue-green (working) would have to move to Bone |
| E. **Searchlight white** (not red) | `#fff6f0` core, as on the lit disc | — | The in-film Signal is white light (§8). It is the strongest source, but white is already the text colour (`--bone`), so it cannot be the Alarm color on its own; listed for completeness and not recommended |

The hex values for B and D are ours and their fills are not yet checked as large-text inks. The steel-blue ground and the mercury and sodium secondaries do, together with the fact that nothing is red at rest. **Rule: no red or orange at rest.** That means:
- The wordmark is in `--bone` with a cool glow.
- Focus rings, checked toggles, slider accents and the tab underline move to `--accent` (Bone); text selection to `--raised`.
- The cowl and cape carry no red.

## 4. Type

The trilogy's title lettering is **unidentified**. On the *Dark Knight* one-sheet, "THE DARK KNIGHT" is set in wide geometric-grotesque capitals, letterspaced by roughly a letter's width (observed on the enwiki thumbnail). Fonts In Use has no entry for any of the three films ([search](https://fontsinuse.com/search?terms=dark+knight)). The proposal evokes that width and spacing. It does not copy the logo.

| Role | Family | Licence and package | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display: wordmark, headlines, intro | **Syncopate** 700, caps, 0.18–0.3em tracking (**new**) | Apache-2.0 per Google Fonts [METADATA](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/apache/syncopate/METADATA.pb); `@fontsource/syncopate`, weights 400 and 700 ([API](https://api.fontsource.org/v1/fonts/syncopate)) | "Light in weight with a wide body width, it is a unicase design" ([description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/apache/syncopate/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html)). It is the opposite register to Anton's condensed caps. **Risk:** it is very wide, so "BAT-SIGNAL" needs about 15–16px in the 300px header. Test it before committing |
| Stamps, case numbers (the typewriter's job) | **Big Shoulders Stencil** 700, caps, about 0.06em (**new**; this Theme's alone in the Wave, jury §10.3) | OFL-1.1; `@fontsource/big-shoulders-stencil`, variable 100–900 ([API](https://api.fontsource.org/v1/fonts/big-shoulders-stencil)) | A crate stencil reads as Applied Sciences gear. "Big Shoulders Stencil explores Chicago's histories in work and protest", and the family was "created for the Chicago Design System" ([description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/bigshouldersdisplay/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html)). Chicago stood in for Gotham, so this is our nod to the city, not the films' type. **Risk:** stencil gaps at 11px. Set stamps at 12px or larger and check them on the real window |
| Body, labels, titles | Barlow Semi Condensed (existing) | OFL-1.1 | Unchanged |
| Mono and Night Report body | JetBrains Mono (existing) | OFL-1.1 | The report becomes a printed log (§5) rather than a typed page, so Special Elite leaves this Theme |

**The Stamp as a shape.** In this Theme the Stamp is a stencilled spec label, not an ink stamp. Set it straight (0°, not 2022's −2.5°), in a 1px box in its own ink, with 2px corners and no `bat-wear` noise mask. The word is the same and only the finish changes: issued gear, not a rubber stamp on paper. That is the Type row's difference in §10.

Alternatives:
- **Michroma** (OFL, single weight 400, [API](https://api.fontsource.org/v1/fonts/michroma)) is wide like Syncopate but more sci-fi.
- **Saira Stencil One** and **Allerta Stencil** (both OFL on Fontsource) are other stencils. Allerta was "designed for use in signage" ([description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/allertastencil/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html)).
- **Big Shoulders Display** keeps the Chicago tie for display, but it is condensed, which is 2022's register.
- Every stamp word must fit the rendered width of PERMISSION in Big Shoulders Stencil, which runs narrow. CHECKED IN and NO CONTACT are the longest; the text catalogue measures them (jury §7, #73).
- **IBM Plex Mono** (OFL) would give the report its own face, at the cost of a third new family.

## 5. Voice

**Tone:** a tactical briefing at Wayne Enterprises after hours: Lucius Fox's dry precision and Gordon's radio brevity. Short, factual, a little wry, never gothic and never jokey. Alfred is left out on purpose: his voice in Bruce's ear belongs to the Knightmare Theme (jury §2). It sounds like a log that someone reads aloud. The Voice never renames a term; that is the Lexicon's job (§6).

Sample lines (all ours unless marked):

1. Empty Needs you: **"The roof is dark."** The hint stays "Nothing needs you right now." This avoids Burton's "Gotham sleeps. For now." *Dark* here means all clear, its only meaning in the Wave (jury §4.3).
2. Empty Cases: "No ops running. Get some rest." It names a term, so it takes the word from the Lexicon: with the Lexicon off it reads "No cases running. Get some rest." (jury §7, #73).
3. Night Report dateline: **"SITREP · GOTHAM"** with the 24-hour time, e.g. "SITREP · GOTHAM · 02:14".
4. Night Report heading for what waits on you (was "Awaiting your signature"): "Waiting on you".
5. Night Report heading for running work: "In the field".
6. Night Report close when nothing waits: **"End of report. Darkest just before the dawn."** This is the Theme's one film line, a fragment of Harvey Dent's "The night is darkest just before the dawn. And I promise you, the dawn is coming." ([Wikiquote](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_(film))). Use it only here.
7. Night Report close when something waits: "End of report. You're needed."
8. Settings kicker (was "Bat-Computer"): "Applied Sciences" (a division name from the film, not a line; Wikipedia's plot spells it "Applied Science Division", and the plural is our choice).
9. Atmosphere toggle: "Gotham's lit windows behind the cases".
10. Lit Signal tooltip: "The light's on."

Left out on purpose:
- "Why So Serious?" is the Joker's and the marketing tagline ([Wikiquote](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_(film))).
- "Some men just want to watch the world burn" reads wrong over a stalled job.
- Gordon's "a watchful protector" is now the display name, so it is no longer a hover-title fallback. The Dent fragment stays the Theme's one line inside the panel.

## 6. Lexicon

Frame: an Applied Sciences operations board. The test, as in the Burton dossier: would someone who has never seen the films understand the word on a stamp at 12px, and does it read at least as severe as the standard word? Glossary words (Case, Signal, Stamp) are not reused as themed words. The jury's fixes are adopted (jury §5, §10.7).

| Standard | Themed | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Case | **Op** (plural **Ops**) | The tab says **Ops**: the plural reads as operations, where a lone "op" can read as an operator or an opcode. Fall back to **Operation** if it is misread |
| Needs you | **On you** | Clear: the next move is on you. *Calls* was dropped: it reads as phone calls or function calls |
| Permission | **CLEARANCE** | Clear in this frame. Kept by the jury. The 2022 dossier rejected it for the standard set as "slower to read". (*AUTHORIZE* is no longer a fallback: it is the Knightmare Theme's word) |
| Error | **FAULT** | Clear and at least as severe. Kept. Rejected: **BREACH**, which suggests a security incident |
| Waiting | **YOUR MOVE** | Clear: it says the next step is the user's. Kept |
| New reply | **CHECKED IN** | Clear: Claude has reported back. *REPORT IN* was dropped: it reads as an order to the user, and it doubled "report" inside the Night Report |
| Stalled | **NO CONTACT** | Clear: nothing heard. *GONE DARK* was dropped: in this app *dark* means all clear, and it could read as "ended" |

The Signal shows the count and, on its notice card, the stamp word. Windows notifications and the tray keep the standard words (`CONTEXT.md` → Lexicon).

## 7. Bat-Clawd

> **Superseded by [`bat-clawd-nolan-suit.md`](bat-clawd-nolan-suit.md)**, approved on the prototype (#95): the black screen suit with lit plates, the smoked-bronze belt, the sculpted chest bat and the three-point batwing glide, under the Bat-Clawd Costume Rule in `DESIGN.md`. The steel collar and cheek guards below are not in the film's costume.

The grid follows `BatClawd.tsx`: body `[3,3,10,6]`, eyes at y = 3, arms at y = 6, legs at y = 9–10, viewBox −6…22 × −1…12. The orange body and white eyes stay. **No red on the mascot at rest**, because the suit is "non-reflective" and the cape "absolutely matte black" ([ICG](https://www.icgmagazine.com/web/july-cover-story-the-dark-knight/)).

Every identifying detail is at least 1 grid unit (about 2.1px at the header's 60px), the Wave rule the jury set after measuring the first draft's 0.2–0.6-unit details (jury §4, §6).

**Cowl** (the 2008 helmet-like cowl with a separate neck):
- Ears: `[4.5, 0, 1, 1]` and `[10.5, 0, 1, 1]`, half a cell further in than 2022's tips at x = 4 and 11. At a full unit wide they no longer count as a silhouette difference from 2022; the collar, the cheek guards and the straight hem carry it.
- Crown `[4, 1, 8, 1]` and the face band `[3, 2, 10, 3]` keep their footprint.
- Cheek guards: `[3, 5, 1, 1]` and `[12, 5, 1, 1]` in cowl black, a full cell below the band, after the "motorcycle helmet" mask ([Special effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_effects_of_The_Dark_Knight)).
- **Collar band:** `[4, 5, 8, 1]` in steel `#3a4754`, between the cheek guards, after the "neckpiece … came up to meet the cowl" (same source). It reads as a helmet sitting on a neckpiece. The first draft's 0.3-tall seam was under a pixel.
- `COWL_EDGE` is stroked in steel `#3a4754` instead of Signal Red. `COWL_SHINE` stays at `[5, 1, 2, 1]` in `#2a3644`.

**Chest emblem** (new part):
- The film's chest bat is black on black and sculpted in relief (observed on the enwiki [*Begins* publicity still](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Bale_as_Batman.jpg), low resolution). At 16×11 a relief can't show, so it is drawn in cowl black on the orange as the emblem's two defining features.
- The flat top line: `[5.8, 7, 4.4, 1]`, a full unit tall and one row lower than the first draft, so a row of orange separates it from the collar.
- The V tail: `[7.5, 8, 1, 1]`. At a full unit it may read as a T; if it does at 60px, drop it and keep the top line alone (jury §6).

**Gauntlets** (new): the outer arm pixels `[1,6]` and `[14,6]` turn cowl black, after the finned gauntlets, which the 2008 suit made "retractable and launchable" ([Special effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_effects_of_The_Dark_Knight)). The first draft's 0.5×0.4 fins are dropped: under a unit, they don't read.

**Cape:** matte `#05080b`. The hem is **straight**, not jagged: replace the zigzags in `wrapped` and `openLeft` with a flat bottom edge at y ≈ 10.5–11. `threadLeft` is dropped. In its place, a 0.25-wide `--ash` rim at 35% runs along the outer edge only: Pfister's "very soft backlight" ([AC 2005](https://theasc.com/article/batman-begins-the-bat-takes-wing/)).

**Signature pose: "Sonar".**
- **The scene it honours.** The climax of *The Dark Knight*, where Fox's city-wide surveillance network, which "helped Batman find the Joker" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight)), is seen through the cowl's glowing white lenses. The lens look is from the film and **unverified** in writing.
- **Why not the alternatives.** The poster stance (upright, cape closed) overlaps 2022's front-on stand. The glide is used for flying. The sonar is the trilogy's alone among the Wave.
- **Standing, not crouched.** The jury asked for a crouch (body 1–2 rows lower, cape pooled wide) only if a source supports it (§4.2, §10.8). Two searches found none: the written accounts of the Prewitt building climax describe the sonar tracking the Joker and the fight at the top, and the closest ledge moment found (before the glide into Lau's building) has Batman standing ([Movie Mistakes](https://moviemistakes.com/film6359/page3)). So he stands, and the ring carries the pose. Its outline differs from 2022's front-on stand through the ring, the lens glow, the collar and the straight hem.
- **The drawing.**
  - He stands front-on, legs as today, arms down at the sides: `[2, 6, 1, 2]` and `[13, 6, 1, 2]`.
  - The cape hangs straight behind him, one cell showing on each side, flat hem at y = 10.5.
  - The eyes stay white `EYES_OPEN`, at full `--bone`, with a static lens glow behind them: `[4.6, 2.7, 6.8, 1.6]` in `--bone` at 25%.
- **The ping.** One ring in `--bone`, stroke 0.25, centred on (8, 3). Over 4 ticks of the shared clock, its radius goes 4 → 8 → 12 → 16 and its opacity 40% → 28% → 16% → 6%. Then it is gone.

  - It plays in the intro and on click, in place of the 0.7s hop.
  - On the perch it plays once per 12-step `WATCH` cycle, in place of the cape gust (the cape is heavy). That costs 4 ring redraws plus the existing glances per 12 s, about 6 redraws.
  - Reduced motion shows the glow without the ring.
- **Caution:** keep it to one ring. A blue wireframe sonar overlay would read as the Arkham games' detective mode (§10).

## 8. Emblem

**Reference.** The shape is corroborated by the *Dark Knight* one-sheet's title-logo bat ([enwiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:The_Dark_Knight_(2008_film).jpg)), but that copy is too small to measure. So the numbers come from a photograph of a Bat-Signal projected onto Fifth Avenue Place in Pittsburgh during the *Rises* shoot: [Brian Donovan, 28 July 2011, CC BY-SA 2.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Batsignal_at_Highmark_building.jpg). [Patch](https://patch.com/pennsylvania/upperstclair/bat-signal-glows-in-downtown-pittsburgh) places it "during filming in downtown Pittsburgh" and says the light changed between neon green and white. Whether it was a shooting prop or a promotional projection is **unverified**. The photo is taken from below and the outline is a glowing stroke, so perspective and stroke width bias the numbers. Treat them as a draft, to be rechecked against a frontal reference.

**Shape** (measured on the photo's stroke centre line and made symmetric by us):
- **Proportions:** about **3.7:1**, width to height. Much flatter than 2022's.
- **Top edge:** a straight, level line across the outer two-thirds of each half (about 64% of the half-width). Then it dips in a smooth curve toward the head, to about 23% of the height, right beside the ears. **The wings sit higher than the head.**
- **Ears:** short, blunt and upright, close together (their outer edges about 8% of the width apart). Their tips stop about 3% of the height below the wing line. The head between them is a flat or slightly rounded top at about 14% height.
- **Wing tips:** the top-outer corners are long, sharp points aimed straight outward. From each tip, the outer edge runs inward and gently down for about a third of the half-width, reaching about 35% height. Then it turns almost vertical, down to a sharp lower-outer corner at about 59% height. This is one hook per side. **There are no scallops on the lower edge.**
- **Lower edge:** nearly straight and level from the lower-outer corner inward, to about 30% of the half-width from the centre. Then it bends into a **sharp V tail**: the deepest point, on the centre line, at 100% height.
- It is symmetric, so draw the left half and mirror it at x = 60, as `BatEmblem.tsx` does.

**In the 120×48 box:** the full width gives 120 × 32.5, centred (y = 8 to 40.5). This draft path is traced by us and rendered to check it reads as the trilogy bat:

- **Tray and toast icons** stay single, drawn from the default emblem, whatever the Theme. The jury ruled this for Burton (§2); it applies here too, so the 3.7:1 bat never has to fit a 16px tray icon.

**The 64px Signal disc:**
- **Unlit:** dark steel glass, a radial gradient from `--surface` at the centre to `--abyss` at the rim. A 1px `--line` rim. The emblem is a faint `--raised` silhouette at 72% of the disc's width. No warm color.
- **Lit:** the in-film signal is white light with a black bat ([the 2011 photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Batsignal_at_Highmark_building.jpg)). The disc still has to carry the Alarm, so the lens is a searchlight that burns at the edge:
  - Radial gradient: `#fff6f0` at 0%, `#ffd2bd` at 25%, `--signal-hot` at 55%, `--signal` at 78%, `--blood` at 100%.
  - The bat in `--abyss` across the white core.
  - Halo: `0 0 18px var(--signal-glow)`.
  - The count is a Barlow 700 numeral on `--abyss`, in a `--signal-hot` box.
  - Compared with 2022: there, the bat is a shadow in a red lens. Here, a white-hot core is ringed in fire.

## 9. Atmosphere

**"Lit windows": a modernist skyline lit from within.** This comes from Pfister's rule that city light comes from inside buildings ([AC 2005](https://theasc.com/article/batman-begins-the-bat-takes-wing/)) and from Crowley's clean modernist blocks (§2).

- **Skyline:** a static band along the bottom of the panel, about 20% of its height. Four or five flat-roofed slab towers in `--smoke`, edged with 1px `--line`, with no spires or gothic detail. Each carries a curtain-wall grid of 3×4px window cells with 1px mullions. Draw it once to a canvas (or a static SVG), and redraw it only on resize.
- **Windows:** about 8% of cells are lit, from a seeded random choice:
  - 70% fluorescent white (`--bone` at 22%).
  - 30% mercury (`--ink-live` at 28%).
  - No sodium and never `--signal`: the no-red-or-orange-at-rest rule (§3) covers the Atmosphere too, so amber stays in the waiting stamps.
- **Motion:** on every 8th tick of the shared 8 fps clock (about once a second), switch one cell on or off by drawing a single 3×4 rect. That is one tiny repaint per second, with no full-canvas redraw. It pauses when the window is hidden. Reduced motion or the animations switch off leaves the windows frozen. Nothing goes on the transparent Signal window.
- **Haze:** replaces the red sodium glow. A static gradient from the bottom edge, `rgb(124 198 196 / 6%)` to transparent: mercury light from the street.
- **Grain:** keep it static, lowered to about 3%. The AC article stresses the IMAX "sharper and clearer" image.
- **Rejected:**
  - Embers: the Alarm color at rest.
  - Snow: Burton's Atmosphere, and *Rises* is secondary here.
  - A sonar wireframe overlay: busy, and too close to Arkham.
  - A sweeping searchlight beam: it reads as an alert.

## 10. Distinctiveness

Against **The Batman (2022)**:

| Axis | The Batman (2022) | WATCHFUL PROTECTOR (Nolan) |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | Pure black and reds. Red is the light, the stamp and the cowl edge. Sodium amber means working | Blue-black and steel. **Nothing warm at rest.** An alarm decided on screen (fire red-orange in the draft) means "needs you", mercury blue-green means working, sodium amber means waiting |
| Type | Anton condensed caps; Special Elite stamps, crooked and ink-worn | Syncopate **wide**, letterspaced caps; Big Shoulders Stencil stamps as straight spec labels with no wear; mono report |
| Bat-Clawd silhouette | Red-edged cowl, jagged hem, red thread; ribbed wingsuit; draped, front-on Signature | Steel-edged helmet cowl with a full-unit collar and cheek guards, black gauntlets, a 1-unit flat-topped chest bat; **straight hem**; emblem-shaped rigid glide; "Sonar" lenses and ping |
| Atmosphere | Diagonal rain, red haze, film grain | Static modernist skyline whose windows switch on and off; mercury haze; lighter grain |
| Voice | Bruce's night journal, case file, bleak | Operations briefing by Fox and Gordon: Ops, On you, SITREP, "Applied Sciences"; dry, procedural |

The jury scored this Theme 4 of 5 against 2022: the Silhouette was close, because the first draft's details were under a pixel. The full-unit collar, cheek guards and chest bat above are the fix. Against Burton it scored 5. Against the Knightmare it scored 2 while both dossiers used the ops frame; with the frame now Nolan's alone, the Knightmare dossier expects 5 (`jury-wave-1.md` §1; [`snyder.md`](snyder.md) §10).

**Overlap risks** (the descriptions of other properties are from general knowledge, not researched here; **unverified**):
- **The Arkham games: medium.** Armoured grey and black suits and a blue-teal "detective mode" overlay. Shared ground: steel blue and the sonar idea. Mitigation:
  - Keep the ground near-black, not mid-grey.
  - Keep the suit black.
  - The sonar is a single one-off ring, never a wireframe tint.
  - Keep the Nolan bat's flat top line and V tail.
- **The Knightmare Theme (Snyder): low, after the jury.** It now gives up the operations frame, Big Shoulders, the mono log and Alfred, and becomes a near-monochrome desert resistance with Bone as its working ink. What it shares with this Theme is a red alarm family; the grounds (blue steel against neutral grey) and the inks (mercury and sodium against Bone and dust) carry the difference. Keep this chest bat small and the inks saturated.
- **Inside the Wave:**
  - The Burton dossier uses steel blue `#8fb0d3` only as its working ink and warns Nolan off "steel blue as the dominant color". This Theme *is* the steel-blue Theme, so Burton keeps its warm smog and gold.
  - The glide overlap is covered in §7.
  - Resolved: GONE DARK became NO CONTACT and Burton's GONE COLD became NO WORD (jury §4.3).

## 11. Legal note

- **Trademark (DC):** the bat symbol in this version's shape, the names "Batman", "Gotham" and "The Dark Knight", and the films' title treatments.
  - Credit the emblem as `PRODUCT.md` does for 2022: "the bat symbol is a trademark of DC, used here as fan art". Update the README and NOTICE for the new shape.
  - The display name WATCHFUL PROTECTOR is a two-word fragment of Gordon's closing line, used as an allusion. The credit names the films nominatively ("after Christopher Nolan's *Dark Knight* trilogy"). Neither is set in the films' logo lettering. With the Dent fragment (§5), these are the whole of the films' words in this Theme.
- **Copyright, to avoid:**
  - Vehicles. The Ninth Circuit held the Batmobile is a copyrightable "character" ([*DC Comics v. Towle*, 9th Cir., 23 Sept. 2015](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2015/09/23/13-55484.pdf); summary at [Harvard Cyberlaw Clinic](https://clinic.cyber.harvard.edu/2015/09/25/dc-comics-wins-copyright-protection-for-batmobile/)). The case concerned the 1966 and 1989 cars, not the Tumbler, but the reasoning covers the character across its versions. So draw no Tumbler and no Batpod.
  - The poster composition: the burning bat on the tower is specific artwork. The fire is a palette source, not an image to recreate.
  - Stills and frames, including the 2011 photo. That photo is CC BY-SA 2.0, but we only measured it and never ship it.
  - The score: no Zimmer or Howard motif in app sounds.
  - Actors' likenesses: Bat-Clawd's face stays Clawd's, and there is no Joker imagery.
  - Long dialogue: one fragment at most (§5).
- **Style, free to use:** a cold blue-green grade, modernist architecture, stencil lettering, wide letterspaced caps, a tactical briefing frame. Our own words: Ops, On you, SITREP, Clearance.
- **Fictional names:** "Applied Sciences" is used as a plain heading. Don't use "Wayne Enterprises" as a masthead or logo.
- **Fonts:** Syncopate (Apache-2.0) and Big Shoulders Stencil (OFL-1.1). Bundle their licence texts with the existing Fontsource ones. Apache-2.0 also expects the licence and any NOTICE to travel with the font.
