# Research note: SAME BAT-TIME, the pop pass (iconic scenes, pop colour, what could live in the app)

Research note, 2026-10-29. Scope: the maintainer saw the draft of SAME BAT-TIME (GitHub #148, Linear LUC-89: the credits navy `#040e21` / `#011c3a` / `#012a54`, the violet satin alarm `#8a3fe0` / `#bb76ff`, the green waiting ink, soft night clouds under a pale Bat-Signal) and found it too gloomy for Adam West: a comic Theme should be prettier and travel further. They asked to see four iconic scenes (the fight cards, the bat wipe, the labelled Batcave, the Bat-climb) and for more candidates. This note verifies the series' iconic scenes and devices, measures their colours, lists bright grounds with their contrast and their collisions, and says how each could live in the app. Everything is reopenable, the dark ground included. Read with the dossier [`adam-west.md`](adam-west.md), [`same-bat-time-build.md`](same-bat-time-build.md), the reference index [`references/adam-west.md`](references/adam-west.md), `DESIGN.md` (Themes, the Alarm Color Rule, the Atmosphere's variables) and `CONTEXT.md`.

Provenance key, as in [`dkr-rework.md`](dkr-rework.md): **Read** = seen by us on a reference frame, named by its number (`[63]`), or read in a source named beside it. **Measured** = taken off a frame by a script (colour fields, coverage per frame, camera roll). **Inferred** = our reading of what those facts imply. **P** = proposed by us. Frames [01]–[60] are the dossier's and the build note's; [61]–[100] are new (§2). ΔE00 is CIEDE2000 and contrast is WCAG 2, by `tools-west-iconic/colour.py` (it reproduces the juries' 14.6 for `#ff405f`–`#e3121b` and the dossier's 12.00 for Bone on `--raised`).

## 1. TL;DR

- **The series' pop has two halves, and only one is free.** Measured off the titles, the fight cards and the lairs: a **hot half** (red `#ae000c`–`#ff0100`, orange `#d76437`–`#f37531`, coral `#ca6461`, magenta `#a22466`, the lairs' pink and magenta gels `#fd4ecc` / `#6b3452`) and a **cool half** (the titles' green `#486c30`–`#4e7739`, the fight cards' green `#17753c` / `#239056`, the henchmen's teal `#287063`, the cliffhanger card's ultramarine `#0359e9`, the credits' cel blue `#1c4781`, ZAP's sky blue `#36acd6`). The hot half is every shipped Theme's alarm territory (VENGEANCE's red 1.1 from the season 3 red, HOLIDAY's orange 4.8 from the bat wipe, Lego's draft magenta 5.5 from the cast card's) and hotter than our violet alarm, which the Alarm Color Rule forbids at rest. **The cool half is ours to use.**
- **A bright ground is possible only as a backdrop, not under text.** On the mid-tone pop fields (L* 38–55) Bone passes 4.5:1 only at L* ≤ 43 and black only at L* ≥ 53, so one ground can carry one text colour and nothing else: Ash, the violet alarm and the green ink all fail (§5.2). And `DESIGN.md` says no Theme's ground is light. So: **dark cards, pop behind them**, as DARK DECO's red sky sits behind its cards.
- **The ground can still lose its gloom.** P: **U2, the cliffhanger ultramarine pulled down to a deep ground**, `#050f45` / `#0a2272` / `#13318f` (surface L* 18, **C* 54, 2.4 times the draft navy's 23**). It is the blue of the cliffhanger cards [34] rather than of the night sky. Costs, Measured: the alarm lifts to `#c995ff` (4.95:1 on raised, 7.8 from the draft's) and Ash to `#b4b2cc`; the abyss sits 8.1 from BLUE COLLAR's raised. Nearest surface 12.7 (WATCHFUL PROTECTOR's and BLUE COLLAR's raised).
- **Iconic devices, verified** (§3): the fight words (overlaid on the action in season 1 [13]–[15], then full-frame cards on flat colour [27]–[30], per a fan source and Read); the bat wipe ("a Batman logo appearing over a rapidly-spinning background", Wikipedia) [61] [62]; the Bat-climb with 14 window cameos, shot on a wall laid on its side, gone in season 3 [63]–[65]; the cliffhanger cards and the narrator's line [82] [83]; the Dutch tilt in the villains' lairs (Wikipedia; **Measured** 5–8° in the Riddler's hideout [100], under 2° elsewhere); the labelled Batcave and gadgets ("PARACHUTE JETTISON BUTTON", "OCEANIC REPELLENT BAT SPRAYS", a van marked "BATMOBILE PARACHUTE PICKUP SERVICE") [67] [71] [73] [79]; the red hotline [74]; the Batmobile, Batcopter, Batboat, Batcycle [68]–[69] [76]–[78] [94]; the bomb [95]; the Shakespeare bust [75]; Batgirl and the next-villain tag in season 3 [89].
- **The bat wipe, measured:** an orange bat grows from the centre, its cover doubling about every 0.12 s, from 1% to 80% of the frame in **0.7 s**, then a hard cut [61]; the spinning version zooms to **99% in 0.6 s and back out in 0.4 s** [62]. At 8 fps: 6 and 8 ticks.
- **Ranked for the build** (end of note): (1) the cliffhanger ultramarine as the ground; (2) a still backdrop of the titles' cool colour fields, the cast card's city or the Signal on a pop sky; (3) labels as plates (the Batcave's lettering) on the existing caption variables; (4) the bat wipe as a one-off transition, in the oval's yellow; (5) the cliffhanger card in the Voice; (6) more POW!: the balloon's colour pairs and the alarmed burst; (7) the Bat-climb as the flying pose; (8) the tilt on what needs you.
- **The strongest primary source** is the series' cinematographer's own account, *American Cinematographer*, June 1966 ([ASC](https://theasc.com/articles/bat-matography-or-capturing-batman-on-film)): fights staged for the words' "later superimposure", the tilt only "in the lair of one of the villains", a gel colour per villain (Catwoman amber, the Riddler green, the Penguin purples), and the Bat-climb shot on a wall laid on its side.
- **Unverified:** the spinning newspaper headlines (no clip or source), the title animator (the titles are uncredited), the closing credits' villain cameos, the Joker's gel, the exact Batcave labels "ATOMIC BATPILE" and "BAT-ANALYZER", most episode attributions (§8).

### 4.2 The bat wipe's timing

`wipe.py` counts the orange's share of each frame at 25 fps (Measured):
- **[61], the grow-and-cut:** 1% at 70.40 s, 10% at 70.84, 23% at 71.00, 47% at 71.12, 74% at 71.24, 80% at 71.32, then 0% at 71.36 (the cut to the climb). The cover doubles about every 0.12 s: the bat grows evenly in size, so its area grows fast at the end. **0.7 s from a speck to the cut.**

### 4.3 The tilt

`tilt.py` (Measured): the roll of each frame from its near-vertical edges. The Riddler's hideout: +7.8° (8 s, 12 s), +5.2° (24 s), +4.8° (36 s, 40 s) [100]; its death-trap shots are level but shot from below [81]. ### 5.2 What a bright ground costs

- **One text colour per ground.** On every mid-tone field (L* 38–55), only one of Bone or black reaches 4.5:1, and often barely (the fight-card green 4.81, the cliffhanger ultramarine 4.86, SPLATT green 4.89 for black). Ash, the violet alarm and the green ink fall to 1.0–3.5:1 on them (`ultra.py` and `grounds.py`; e.g. the alarm fill `#8a3fe0` reads 1.04–1.85 on the green, orange, coral and pink fields). A Theme's 11 grounds each carry five text colours today (`themeContrast.test.ts`), so a bright panel would need its stamps, counts and quiet text redrawn in the one passing ink.
- **The rule.** `DESIGN.md`, the Pure Black Ground Rule: "Other Themes may set a dark near-black ground of their own; none is ever light." A bright panel ground reopens a Named Rule for the whole app, not one Theme.
- **Clawd's jaw.** On the orange field Clawd's orange `#d77757` is 9.3 ΔE00 and 1.05:1 away; on coral 10.6 and 1.21:1 (Measured). His jaw would vanish.
- **The alarm.** The hot fields are hotter than the violet alarm, which must be the hottest colour in the Theme (`CONTEXT.md`, Alarm color); and red, orange, coral and yellow are other Themes' alarms within 1–7 ΔE00 (table). Only the cool half (greens, teal, ultramarine, credits blue) leaves the alarm alone, and the bright blues are ANCIENT GRACE's alarm (1.5–2.0).

So the pop fields belong **behind the cards** (the Atmosphere's backdrop, with `--bare-veil` under words that sit on it, DARK DECO's method), and the ground itself can only get **more saturated, not lighter**.

## 7. Legal line, per idea

Homage under ADR 0001 and the dossier's §12. Generic and free: flat colour fields, starbursts and onomatopoeia, label plates, a tilted camera, a cliffhanger question, a spinning transition as a technique. **Not used:** the series' logo [01] [96], its title drawings and cards (the fields are sampled for colour only), any frame, the theme music and the narrator's recordings, the likeness of Adam West, Burt Ward, Yvonne Craig or any guest of the window cameos, the Batmobile's, Batcopter's and Batboat's designs (DC's), the Bat-Shield, "Holy …". The bat in a wipe is drawn by us and credited as every Theme's emblem is. Label texts are ours ("Bat-Settings"); we do not reuse the show's product names as labels (BATPHONE is a live DC mark: the build note §7).
