# References: Batman: The Long Halloween (Loeb, Sale, Wright, 1996–97)

For the dossier `docs/research/themes/long-halloween.md` (Wave 3, round 1, LUC-85 / LUC-87). Gathered 2026-10-22. These images are copyrighted art. They stay in a scratch folder, outside the repository, for our sampling and for the maintainer's boards, and are never committed, traced or shipped (ADR 0001); only this list of sources is committed.

Raw downloads (the Back Issue #60 PDF, unused crops, fan-wiki files not kept) are in `../dl-tlh/`. Scripts are in `../tools-tlh/` and were run with `python -I` from that folder.

**Editions, and why they matter.** The colours differ between reproductions of the same page (compare 16 and 31). Every entry names what it is a reproduction of, as far as we can tell:
- **Covers 02–14:** 400px scans of the printed single issues, cover dates December 1996 to December 1997 (DC Database). Which printing is not stated.
- **Textless covers 23–27:** the cover art without logos, from DC Database. Source edition not stated (probably a collected edition's cover gallery). Their colour differs from the printed covers (compare 10 and 24).
- **15–18:** *Back Issue* #60 (TwoMorrows, October 2012), whose caption says the Long Halloween art it reproduces "features Gregory Wright's colors". Magazine reprints, 2012.
- **20–22:** DC.com's own images for its 2025 blog post. Probably current digital files.
- **31–34:** a 2022 blog post; edition not stated. 34 matches 21 closely, so probably the same current digital source. 31 is the same panel as 16 and its colour differs (see the dossier, §3).
- **35–36:** DC Database panel scans; issue named by the wiki, edition not stated.

**The five that best say what the book looks like:** 15 (black, wine blinds, the long ears), 22 (the cape and the calendar), 02 (the jack-o'-lantern and the cover design), 10 (Batman as Sale and Wright colour him), 33 (the Signal and the rain).

Not used: the 2021 animated films, the 2021 *Long Halloween Special*, *The Last Halloween* (2024–25) and *Dark Victory* (a Calendar Man cover from *Dark Victory* #7 was downloaded and dropped). Fan art: none.
