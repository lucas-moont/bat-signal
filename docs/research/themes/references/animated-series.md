# References: Batman: The Animated Series (1992)

Index for the Theme dossier `docs/research/themes/animated-series.md` (LUC-85, Wave 3, round 1). Gathered 2026-10-22. The images it lists stay in a scratch folder and are never committed, traced or shipped (ADR 0001); only this list of sources is committed.

**Source of images 1–54:** The World's Finest (dcanimated.com), a fan reference site that hosts Warner Bros. Animation material: frames of the opening and closing titles (Intro 1, as aired from 1992), Bruce Timm's model sheets, colour models, production backgrounds with their slates, production and promotional cels, the pre-launch style guide ("sent to media partners before the series' official launch", per the page), and the 2018 HD remaster's title cards. No fan art is used. **Images 55–58 are official** (DC's own shop, and Warner Bros. Home Entertainment's press image), added 2026-10-22 so the suit and title-card claims have an official check; the HD title cards [42]–[50] are captures of the official 2018 Warner Bros. Home Entertainment Blu-ray, but the captures are hosted by The World's Finest, not by Warner. *Batman Animated* (Dini and Kidd, 1998) was not obtained: no authorised online copy was found, and an unauthorised scan was not used. Only the original series is used: nothing from *The New Batman Adventures* (1997). The *Adventures of Batman & Robin* (1994) retitle keeps the 1992 designs and is not used here either.

**Edition notes:** the opening frames are 640x480 captures (an SD video source, likely the DVD). The title cards are 1440x1080 captures of the 2018 HD remaster, regraded, so their colour is not the 1992 broadcast. The cels and colour models are photographs or scans of painted cels at different exposures (the same grey reads L* 26 on one and L* 60 on another), so they inform hue, not lightness. The backgrounds are scans of the painted boards.
